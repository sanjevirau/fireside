incoming bytes. Copy, export, and import preserve it. Downloads retain gzip bytes
and encoding when accepted; otherwise they stream decompressed bytes without
encoding/length and ignore Range, matching the official capture. The differential

invoke its object-header helper on that path. Metadata and exports retain the
field. The implementation and DESIGN follow the captured behavior, not a guessed
header rule. The tiny Firebase resumable exchange is explicitly labeled as a
protocol probe because the SDK chooses multipart for this payload size.
